Is this fleet the right size?

A dispatch planner for one distributor in Quezon City. It plans a full day of deliveries with the fleet from my Part 1 recipe, then shows whether there are too few or too many vehicles. All data is made up for the demo.

The situation

One distributor delivers to about 600 sari-sari stores, minimarts and grocers. Each day, some of them order.

The question

Does the distributor have enough vans and trucks to deliver every order on time, without paying for vehicles that sit idle?

How to read it

  • About right: every delivery made, at most 1 spare (the backup)
  • Too small: some stores miss their delivery
  • Too big: more vehicles idle than the 1 backup

About this data

Everything here is synthetic, made up to look like a Quezon City distributor. The full list is in docs/part2/data-assumptions.md.

  • Refrigerated van (A): carries up to 1,000 kg, costs ₱2,500 a day plus ₱35 per km.
  • Dry truck (B): carries up to 4,000 kg, costs ₱3,000 a day plus ₱25 per km.
  • Working day: 7 AM to 5 PM. Each store has a delivery time window.
  • Time at each stop: 15 minutes at a sari-sari store, 30 at a grocer, plus 5 minutes on peak days.
  • Traffic: 18 km/h on normal days, 14 km/h on peak days. Road distance is estimated as 1.4 times the straight line.
  • Fleet sizes come from my Part 1 recipe, run on five years of made-up order history.
